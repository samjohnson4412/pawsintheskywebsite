## The service (what the site promises)

All from Sam's quote email to a customer, 2025-05-13 ("Flying with Cats?"):

- Pets travel "under our feet in the cabin, not in cargo".
- "GPS tracking and photo updates throughout the entire trip."
- "Full trip delay insurance. If we are delayed and have to spend the night
  somewhere, you do not have to pay anything extra."
- "Confirmed flights, so you will know the exact flight number and arrival time."
- Airport drop-off and pick-up as standard; door-to-door for an added fee.
- Two larger cats needed "two carriers and couriers" per airline size rules.
- Health certificate (CVI) "within 10 days of travel"; rabies vaccination.
- Prices hold if booked "more than 2 weeks in advance".

From the **Flight Nanny** Google Form (used since 2022) and its responses:

- What Sam asks: species, breed, birthdate, weight, height and length,
  medication, health, health certificate, vocal 1–10, "Will your pet let
  strangers hold him/her? This is necessary as we go through security",
  aggression, potty training, car sickness, diet, airport drop-off/pick-up,
  extra phone numbers for updates, payment method, photo consent.
- Terms: deposit due within 23 hours of accepting ($100 or more depending on
  airline); "not refundable" except "in the event of your pet becoming ill …
  I will fully refund the deposit"; balance due on delivery, cash accepted;
  Zelle, Venmo, Apple Pay, PayPal or card; CitizenShipper's fee "is not part
  of my quote".
- Responses run from October 2022 to July 2025, mostly 8–16-week-old puppies
  from breeders to buyers. Hence "most of our trips" on the site.

From **FlightNanny.docx** (per-trip checklist): size confirmed, 1-week text,
1-day text, weather check, road check — hence the pre-travel check-ins.

## Rules stated as general fact

- 8 weeks and weaned: USDA Animal Welfare Act transport regulations for dogs
  and cats.
- TSA screens pets out of the carrier, held by the handler.
- Airlines set in-cabin carrier dimensions and their own minimum ages.
